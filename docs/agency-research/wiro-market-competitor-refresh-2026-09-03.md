3. Audience and offer hypotheses

H1 — Hebrew-speaking Israeli travelers in Chiang Mai who want a private day or multi-day experience but need help reducing kosher/Shabbat planning uncertainty.
Evidence: E1, E2, E10, E11, E12. Hypothesis signals to test: Hebrew-language search/click demand, inquiries mentioning Shabbat or kosher, and requests for Hebrew support. Do not state audience size without first-party analytics or a documented research source. Confidence: medium.

H2 — Families and private groups seeking a flexible pace, private vehicle and route customization rather than a fixed group itinerary.
Evidence: E4, E5, E6, E7. Test signals: group size, children’s ages, desired pace, pickup needs and requested destinations. Confidence: medium.

H3 — Adventure-oriented travelers looking for less standard countryside/off-road access, but who still need clear safety, comfort and logistics information.
Evidence: E8 and WIRO’s indexed off-road positioning in E1–E3. Test signals: route interest, mobility/comfort concerns, footwear/vehicle questions and willingness to customize. Confidence: low-to-medium; no demand or willingness-to-pay data is available.

Offer hypotheses (not approved offers or prices):

- “Private Chiang Mai 4x4 day trip with Hebrew/English planning support” — test as a service description, not a guarantee.
- “Kosher-aware itinerary planning” — specify what WIRO actually coordinates; avoid implying certification or guaranteed kosher provision unless documented.
- “Shabbat-aware trip planning” — show an example planning workflow and boundaries; do not promise religious compliance without client-approved operational facts.
- “Family-paced private route” — explain customization inputs and accessibility/safety limits with verified details.

EN intent hypotheses: “private Chiang Mai 4x4 tour,” “family private tour Chiang Mai,” “custom northern Thailand day trip,” and “kosher-friendly Chiang Mai tour.” These are hypotheses based on public offer language, not keyword-volume findings.

HE intent hypotheses: Hebrew searches for “טיול פרטי בצ'אנג מאי”, “טיולי 4x4 בצ'אנג מאי”, “טיול כשר בתאילנד/צ'אנג מאי”, “בית חב״ד צ'אנג מאי” and “טיול למשפחות בצ'אנג מאי”. These should be validated in Search Console/Keyword Planner before SEO prioritization; no search volumes are claimed.
