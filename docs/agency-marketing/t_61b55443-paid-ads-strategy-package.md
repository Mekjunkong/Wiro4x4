## 1. Objective and offer

Primary objective (proposed): generate qualified tour enquiries that can be answered through the existing inquiry, booking, or WhatsApp paths. A qualified enquiry should include enough information for an operator to assess dates, group, route, and requirements; this is an operational definition, not a current performance claim.

Secondary objective (proposed): learn which high-intent language and route themes produce useful conversations before considering broader prospecting.

Offer hypothesis (not approved): private Chiang Mai / Northern Thailand tour planning for travellers who want a tailored route, with an English/Hebrew experience and kosher-aware planning where operationally supported. The existing owner-supplied planning note says a Hebrew-speaking guide is available, but this and every kosher/service-availability statement must be re-confirmed before publication. No price, discount, availability, review, safety, or guaranteed-outcome claim is included.

CTA drafts (not approved):

- EN: Request an itinerary; Ask about your dates; Start a WhatsApp conversation.
- HE: בקשו הצעת מסלול; שאלו לגבי התאריכים שלכם; התחילו שיחה ב‑WhatsApp.

Service fee and ad spend: Marketing/Studio service fees are separate from any media spend. No spend is authorized or entered by this document. Any budget below is a planning scenario only.

## 2. Audience hypotheses

1. High-intent searchers: people actively searching for private Chiang Mai/Northern Thailand tours and ready to ask about dates. Hypothesis only; no volume, CPC, conversion, or ROAS data is available.
2. Kosher-aware trip planners: people searching for kosher travel/tour arrangements. Use neutral, user-led wording; do not infer or target a person's religion or other sensitive trait.
3. Hebrew-language planners: people searching in Hebrew or seeking Hebrew-language support. Language is an ad/landing experience hypothesis, not a verified audience-size claim.
4. Family/private-trip planners: people seeking a private route for a group. Existing site route `/private-family-tours` supports a landing candidate; suitability of the current copy requires review.

Do not build custom audiences from customer lists, religious identity, health/accessibility inference, or chat content. Geographic targeting, age, language, destinations, and schedule remain unverified account-side decisions requiring approval and operational confirmation.

## 6. Keywords and negatives

Positive themes are hypotheses, not search-volume evidence. Starting negatives (review against search terms before applying): jobs, employment, training, course, free, map, weather, YouTube, DIY, used car. Add car rental only if it is intentionally excluded from this campaign; `/car-rental` is a separate site route.

Do not add broad negatives that could block legitimate kosher, Hebrew, family, or destination intent. Google’s negative-keyword behavior differs from positive matching and requires deliberate match-type review: https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2453972?hl=en (accessed 2026-09-03).

Google match-type reference: https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7478529?hl=en (accessed 2026-09-03).

## 7. Landing-page mapping and funnel

Discovery → relevant route → proof/FAQ/itinerary review → inquiry start or WhatsApp click → operator qualification → quote/proposal → booking. Routes observed in `client/src/App.tsx`: `/`, `/pricing`, `/tours`, `/tours/:slug`, `/packages`, `/book`, `/kosher-tours`, `/hebrew-guide`, `/private-family-tours`, Hebrew intent routes under `/he/`, `/contact`, and `/faq`.

Recommended landing rules:

- Match ad language and intent to route language; preserve Hebrew `dir="rtl"` behavior.
- Keep a single primary CTA per test variant; verify mobile WhatsApp and form behavior.
- Do not add pricing or availability to ads/landing pages unless Mike/client and operations approve the exact values.
- Do not use `/admin`, `/login`, `/register`, `/album/:token`, booking success/cancel, terms, or privacy as ad destinations.

## 8. Measurement plan (future implementation proposal)

Existing source evidence shows privacy-bounded canonical events in `client/src/lib/analytics.ts`, including `commercial_page_view`, `tour_view`, `inquiry_start`, `whatsapp_click`, `booking_start`, and `booking_complete`. `client/src/lib/whatsappAttribution.ts` builds a tracked WhatsApp URL with source code and UTM-derived channel data. This is source verification, not proof that an ad platform receives or imports conversions.

Before any launch, propose to:

1. Obtain Mike/client approval for the event schema, consent behavior, retention, and platforms.
2. Use UTMs such as `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, and approved creative labels; never place names, phone numbers, email, or chat text in URLs/events.
3. QA each event in a staging/test flow and verify Plausible destination, platform diagnostics, deduplication, and attribution without sending real customer data.
4. Define primary events (qualified enquiry and approved WhatsApp conversation) and secondary events (booking start/complete only when the path is verified).
5. Reconcile platform-reported clicks and events with operator-recorded lead quality. No ROAS, revenue, CPA, conversion rate, or incrementality claim is possible without source data.

Meta Pixel reference: https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/meta-pixel (accessed 2026-09-03). Meta Conversions API reference: https://www.facebook.com/business/help/AboutConversionsAPI (accessed 2026-09-03).
