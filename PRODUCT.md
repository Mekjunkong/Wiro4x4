# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Israeli Jewish and kosher-observant travelers visiting Northern Thailand. Three primary personas:

- **Families** (35–45, modern Orthodox): planning adventure trips with kids (ages 5–15), want memorable experiences without compromising kashrut or Shabbat, frustrated by generic group bus tours
- **Post-army explorers** (21–26): traveling in groups of 2–5 friends, want real adrenaline and shareable moments, budget-aware but willing to pay for quality
- **Couples** seeking a unique, romantic adventure with the intimacy of a private tour

Context: planning mode, often on mobile, reading in Hebrew or English (occasionally both in the same session). They arrive through WhatsApp referrals, Hebrew Facebook/Instagram, and direct search in Hebrew.

## Product Purpose

WIRO 4x4 is the first and only kosher off-road 4x4 tour company in Chiang Mai. We run private tours through jungle trails, waterfalls, and hill-tribe villages — with Hebrew-speaking guides, flexible kosher meals, and Shabbat-friendly scheduling.

Success means a traveler lands on the site, immediately understands this is unlike any kosher tour they've seen before, feels confident their observance is handled, and books (or initiates a WhatsApp conversation) within the same session.

## Brand Personality

Warm, adventurous, authentic. The voice is "your Israeli friend who lives in Chiang Mai and knows all the best spots" — enthusiastic and personal, never corporate or lecture-y. Light humor is welcome; kosher and religious topics are always treated with matter-of-fact respect, never trivialized.

## Anti-references

- **Thailand Kosher / Kesher Tours / Koco Travel** — logistics-heavy, corporate, group-bus-tour energy, "traditional Jewish vacation" positioning. These look like travel agencies; we look like a person.
- **Generic adventure tourism sites** — stock photos of anonymous tourists, over-processed HDR landscapes, countdown timers and fake urgency.
- **Budget backpacker aesthetics** — hostel vibes, neon accents, cluttered layouts.
- **Sterile luxury hotel sites** — cold, distant, over-designed, no warmth.

## Design Principles

1. **Adventure leads, kosher follows.** Lead with excitement and raw nature — the off-road imagery, the mud, the waterfalls. Kosher observance is the differentiator that makes this possible for our audience, not the headline.
2. **Specificity over vagueness.** Real trail names (Sticky Waterfalls, Doi Inthanon), real photos of actual guests, real guide names. Specific details build trust faster than any superlative.
3. **Experienced friend, not tour operator.** Every touchpoint — headlines, CTAs, error messages — should feel like a person wrote it, not a marketing department.
4. **Premium without pretension.** High quality without sterility. The brand sits between "boutique adventure" and "personal recommendation from a trusted friend."
5. **Bilingual by default.** Hebrew and English are equals. RTL layouts, Hebrew typography (Heebo), and cultural nuance are first-class requirements — not an afterthought.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Full bilingual support: Hebrew (RTL, Heebo font) and English. Known user needs: mobile-first audience, varied levels of religious observance (never assume one kashrut standard), family users with children present during browsing.
