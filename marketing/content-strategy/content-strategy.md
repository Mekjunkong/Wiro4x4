## Content Pillars

### Pillar 1: Adventure (40% of content)

Show the product. Trails, waterfalls, vehicles, mud, views.

- Hero shots of landscapes
- Action footage of 4x4 on trails
- Before/after trail conditions
- Route previews

### Pillar 2: Trust & Social Proof (25% of content)

Build confidence through other travelers' experiences.

- Customer photos and testimonials
- Google/TripAdvisor reviews
- Family highlight posts
- "Behind the meals" (kosher food prep)

### Pillar 3: Practical Value (20% of content)

Help travelers plan, even if they haven't booked yet.

- Packing lists, best seasons, kosher dining guides
- FAQ answers as content
- Cost estimator promotion
- "How it works" explainers

### Pillar 4: Culture & Connection (15% of content)

Show the human side — guides, local culture, community.

- Guide introductions
- Local community spotlights
- Chabad Chiang Mai collaboration
- Thai-Jewish cultural moments
- Shabbat Shalom posts

## Blog Content Plan (First 3 Months)

### Month 1: Foundation

1. **"The Ultimate Guide to Kosher Travel in Chiang Mai (2026)"**
   - Comprehensive guide: kosher food, Shabbat, Chabad, tours
   - SEO pillar page targeting "kosher travel Chiang Mai"

2. **"5 Off-Road Trails in Chiang Mai You Can't Do by Tour Bus"**
   - Trail descriptions with photos, difficulty levels
   - Position WIRO as the adventure authority

3. **"What to Pack for a 4x4 Adventure in Northern Thailand"**
   - Practical value content
   - Include checklist download option

4. **"Why We Started WIRO 4x4: Our Story"**
   - Brand story, personal, builds connection
   - Founder perspective

### Month 2: Social Proof + Depth

5. **"How the [Family Name] Spent 3 Days Off-Road in Chiang Mai"**
   - Customer story with photos (with permission)
   - Shows what the experience actually feels like

6. **"Kosher Dining Guide: Where to Eat in Chiang Mai"**
   - Practical resource, high search intent
   - Include Chabad info, restaurant details

7. **"Doi Inthanon by 4x4: Thailand's Highest Peak Off the Beaten Path"**
   - Specific trail guide, SEO targeting
   - Beautiful photography

8. **"Best Time to Visit Chiang Mai: A Season-by-Season Guide"**
   - Planning content, evergreen SEO
   - Include weather, crowds, pricing tips

### Month 3: Expand & Convert

9. **"Sticky Waterfalls: Chiang Mai's Most Unique Natural Wonder"**
   - Specific attraction, strong SEO keyword
   - Include "how to visit with WIRO" section

10. **"Group Tours vs. Private 4x4: Why We Don't Do Tour Buses"**
    - Competitive positioning content
    - Contrast with group tour experience

11. **"Hill Tribes of Northern Thailand: A Respectful Guide"**
    - Cultural content, builds authority
    - Address ethical tourism angle

12. **"How to Plan a Kosher Family Adventure in Thailand"**
    - Comprehensive planning guide for families
    - Direct CTA to booking
