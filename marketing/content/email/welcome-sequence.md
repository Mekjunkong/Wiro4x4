## Email Tone Notes

- Always warm and personal, never corporate
- Use "we" and "your" — make it conversational
- Keep paragraphs short (2-3 sentences max)
- Always include WhatsApp as primary CTA alongside email
- Include Hebrew option or bilingual subject lines for Israeli audience
- Never use high-pressure sales language
