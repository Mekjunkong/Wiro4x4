## Brand Story

WIRO 4x4 was born from a simple observation: Jewish travelers visiting Northern Thailand had plenty of temple tours and cooking classes to choose from — but nothing that combined **real off-road adventure** with **genuine kosher observance**. No 4x4 trails through jungle waterfalls with Hebrew-speaking guides. No Shabbat-friendly scheduling. No meals you could trust.

WIRO 4x4 fills that gap. We are the first and only kosher-focused off-road tour company in Chiang Mai — delivering authentic, mud-on-your-boots adventure without asking travelers to compromise on their values.

## Brand Voice & Tone

### Voice (consistent across all channels)

| Dimension                | Position                                | Example                                                                            |
| ------------------------ | --------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Formality**            | Casual-professional                     | "Let's plan your adventure" not "We cordially invite you to inquire"               |
| **Energy**               | Warm and enthusiastic                   | "This is the Thailand most tourists never see" not "We offer tours"                |
| **Humor**                | Friendly, light touches                 | "Mud on your boots, kosher food in your belly" — but never at the expense of faith |
| **Authority**            | Experienced friend, not expert lecturer | "We know these trails — let us show you" not "As the leading provider..."          |
| **Cultural sensitivity** | Always respectful                       | Never trivialize observance, never assume one level of kashrut                     |

### Voice in One Phrase

**"Your adventurous Israeli friend who lives in Chiang Mai and knows all the best spots."**

### Tone Variations by Context

| Context                 | Tone Adjustment                    | Example                                                                  |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Website / marketing     | Warm, inviting, aspirational       | "Discover waterfalls the tour buses can't reach"                         |
| Booking / inquiry       | Helpful, responsive, clear         | "Got it! Here's what I'd recommend for a family of 6..."                 |
| Social media            | Casual, visual-first, storytelling | "Today's trail: 3 hours of jungle, one epic waterfall, zero cell signal" |
| Email confirmation      | Professional, reassuring           | "Your tour is confirmed. Here's everything you need to know."            |
| Reviews / testimonials  | Grateful, personal                 | "Thank you, David! We loved having your family out there."               |
| Kosher/religious topics | Respectful, matter-of-fact         | "All meals meet mehadrin standards" — state clearly, don't over-explain  |

## Visual Identity

### Color Palette

| Color                      | Usage                           | Value                            |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| **Forest Green** (Primary) | Headers, buttons, brand accents | `#1A4D2E` / oklch(0.35 0.08 155) |
| **Gold** (Secondary)       | Accents, highlights, CTAs       | oklch(0.5 0.14 85)               |
| **White**                  | Backgrounds, space              | `#FFFFFF`                        |
| **Dark Forest**            | Body text, foreground           | oklch(0.15 0.01 155)             |

**Why these colors:** Forest green grounds the brand in nature and adventure. Gold adds warmth and a premium feel. The combination conveys "premium outdoor experience" — not budget backpacker, not sterile luxury hotel.

### Typography

| Font                 | Usage                  | Weight Range |
| -------------------- | ---------------------- | ------------ |
| **Playfair Display** | Headlines, hero text   | 400–800      |
| **Poppins**          | Body text, UI elements | 300–700      |
| **Heebo**            | Hebrew content         | 300–700      |

**Why these fonts:** Playfair Display adds elegance and premium positioning for headlines. Poppins is clean and modern for readability. Heebo is the standard choice for beautiful Hebrew typography.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Messaging Do's

- Lead with adventure, support with kosher — we sell excitement first, observance is the differentiator
- Use specific place names (Doi Inthanon, Sticky Waterfalls) — specificity builds trust
- Show real customer photos and testimonials
- Mention Hebrew/WhatsApp availability — it's a major trust signal
- Be direct about pricing structure (base + add-ons) — transparency builds trust
- Use both English and Hebrew where appropriate

### Messaging Don'ts

- Don't lead with religion — we're an adventure company that happens to be kosher
- Don't compare to budget tour companies — we compete on quality and uniqueness
- Don't use "exotic" or "oriental" to describe Thailand — respect the culture
- Don't assume all customers are strictly observant — welcome all levels
- Don't use fear-based messaging ("worried about finding kosher food?") — use empowerment ("enjoy kosher meals on every tour")
- Don't over-promise on specific sightings (wildlife, weather) — nature is unpredictable
- Don't use emojis excessively in formal communications

### Voice Don'ts

- Don't sound corporate ("We are pleased to announce...")
- Don't be preachy about kashrut or observance
- Don't use tourism jargon ("bespoke curated experiences")
- Don't be vague ("amazing tours in beautiful places")
- Don't sound desperate ("Book now before it's too late!")

## Channel Guidelines

### Website

- Primary conversion channel
- Full brand experience — voice, visuals, messaging
- Bilingual (EN/HE) with language switcher
- WhatsApp floating button always visible

### WhatsApp

- Primary communication channel with customers
- Respond within 2 hours during business hours
- Use friendly, personal tone
- Hebrew or English based on customer preference
- Share photos/videos of upcoming tour routes

### Social Media (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok)

- Visual-first: lead with stunning trail/landscape photos and short videos
- Stories/Reels: behind-the-scenes, trail previews, customer highlights
- Feed posts: longer captions with storytelling
- Hashtags: #Wiro4x4 #KosherTravel #ChiangMai #OffRoad #JewishTravel #KosherAdventure
- Post in both English and Hebrew (or alternate)

### Email

- Booking confirmations: professional, clear, all details included
- Newsletter: monthly, mix of trail stories + practical tips + upcoming availability
- Follow-up: personal, reference their specific tour

### Blog

- SEO-focused content about Chiang Mai, kosher travel tips, tour stories
- Bilingual (English + Hebrew)
- 800-1500 words per post
- Include practical information (what to pack, best seasons, kosher dining options)
